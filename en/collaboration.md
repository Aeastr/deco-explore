## Start with a wallpaper

Create or open a saved wallpaper that you want to make together. Tap ![Share](symbol:person.badge.plus) and invite a friend. Starting a shared wallpaper requires [Deco Plus](deco://plus/shared-wallpapers), but friends can join and collaborate for free.

![The top of a creation, with the Share button highlighted beside the blue edit button](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingInvite#crop=3:1&position=top&background=secondary)

## Make room for their ideas

Give your friend editing access if you want them to change the artwork. You can both add stickers and save your changes to the same wallpaper.

## Manage sharing

Once a wallpaper is shared, tap ![Manage Sharing](symbol:person.2) to manage sharing.

![The top of a shared creation, with the two-person sharing button highlighted](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingActive#crop=3:1&position=top&background=secondary)

For a wallpaper you own, choose **Add Participant** to invite someone else or **Sharing Options** to choose who can access it and whether they can edit. You can also copy the link or stop sharing.

![Manage Sharing, showing participants, Sharing Options, Copy Link and Stop Sharing](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingManage)

## Put it on your Lock Screen

In Deco, open [Settings → Shortcuts](deco://settings/shortcuts) and add **Automatically Update Wallpaper**. In Shortcuts, tap **Set Up Shortcut** to configure **Refresh Deco Wallpaper**.

![Refresh Deco Wallpaper in Shortcuts, with the Set Up Shortcut button at the bottom](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingShortcutInstall)

Choose the shared wallpaper you want to refresh, then tap **Next** and finish setup.

![Configure This Shortcut asks which shared Deco wallpaper to refresh each morning, with Beagle Collage selected in the Wallpaper row](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingShortcutChooseWallpaper#crop=3:2&position=top&background=secondary)

Check that the **Get** action uses your shared wallpaper and the **Set Wallpaper** action uses the Photos Lock Screen you want to update. Leave the revision and image size fields empty to use the defaults, and keep **Require Latest Version** on.

![The shortcut gets Beagle Collage as an image and sets it as the Lock Screen wallpaper](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingShortcutActions)

Run the shortcut once to check your choices.

## Keep it up to date

While editing the shortcut, open the sheet at the bottom and search for **Automation**. Choose **Time of Day** to add a schedule directly to this shortcut.

![The bottom sheet in the shortcut editor, with Automation in the search field and Time of Day under Daily Routine](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingAutomationTriggers)

Tap **Time** in the new automation at the top of the shortcut and choose when you want it to run. The example below refreshes the wallpaper at **8:00 AM**. Set up the schedule on each device where you want updates.

![The completed shortcut with an 8:00 AM Time of Day automation above the wallpaper actions](asset:Explore/Collaboration/sharingAutomationReady)

Your friend's changes need to finish syncing to iCloud before a run can use them. If Deco can't verify the latest version, the shortcut leaves your current Lock Screen wallpaper in place.
