Give your wrist a little of the same personality as your wallpaper. A favourite photo, a few stickers or a simple colour palette can become a Photos watch face.

## Design for a glance

Start with a saved wallpaper, or make a design especially for your watch. Choose one clear subject and give it room: small details and long lines of text are harder to read on your wrist.

Leave some quieter space around the time. A simple background can help the clock stand out, while a bold sticker or a familiar photo gives the face its character. Keep important details away from the outer edges, where the rounded screen can crop them.

Try matching the colours to your watch band, or use the same artwork for your iPhone wallpaper and watch face.

## Export your face

Open a saved wallpaper, choose ![Export Wallpaper](symbol:square.and.arrow.up), then select **Apple Watch**. Check the preview before exporting, especially the position of your main subject and any text.

Deco turns your design into a still Photos watch face. Use the sharing destination to share the prepared `.watchface` file, or save it to **Files** for later.

## Add it to your watch

Open the exported file on the iPhone paired with your Apple Watch and follow the prompts to add the face. If you made the design on iPad, send the file to that iPhone first.

Once added, select the face on your watch. Check how the artwork and time look together at wrist size, then adjust the face using the available customization controls.

## Make another version

Keep the saved wallpaper in Deco so you can return to its background, stickers and text. Try a different colour combination, a seasonal detail or a simpler arrangement for a second face.

After changing the artwork, export it again and add the new face. Each exported file captures the design as it looked when you exported it.

You can also send the `.watchface` file to someone else so they can add your design to their own watch.

For help adding a shared face, see [Apple’s watch face guide](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/watch/apdb3107c16a/watchos).
