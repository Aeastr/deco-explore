## Prepare a watch face

Open a saved wallpaper, choose ![Export Wallpaper](symbol:square.and.arrow.down), then select **Apple Watch**. Deco prepares a Photos watch face from your wallpaper.

## Add it on Apple Watch

Add the prepared face to your watch, then edit it and choose when to use it. You can also browse wallpapers in Deco on Apple Watch and tap ![Add Watch Face](symbol:plus) to add a face from there.
