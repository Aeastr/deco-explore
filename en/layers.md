Move stickers together, choose what sits on top, and find stickers hidden behind others.

## Select several stickers

Touch and hold an empty part of the canvas, then drag to draw a selection box. Drag the box across the stickers you want to select, then lift your finger.

![A dashed selection box surrounds the halo and mirror-selfie stickers in a burgundy photo collage while the selection gesture is held.](asset:Explore/Layers/layersSelectionBox)

Drag to move the selected stickers together. Pinch to resize them, or use two fingers to rotate them. Tap an empty part of the canvas to clear your selection.

![The halo and mirror-selfie stickers have separate white selection frames after releasing the selection box, with Group Stickers available in the bottom toolbar.](asset:Explore/Layers/layersSelected)

## Keep stickers together

A group is useful when several stickers make up one part of your design, like a photo and the halo above it.

Select the stickers, then tap ![Group Stickers](symbol:rectangle.3.group) in the toolbar. You can now select, move, resize and rotate them as a group.

![The photo and halo share one white selection frame after grouping, and the toolbar now offers Ungroup Stickers.](asset:Explore/Layers/layersGrouped)

To change one sticker inside a group, select the group, tap **Edit**, then choose the sticker. To separate them again, select the group and tap ![Ungroup Stickers](symbol-asset:custom.rectangle.3.group.slash). Tap empty canvas space to clear the selection before moving a sticker on its own.

## See your layers

Tap ![Layers](symbol:square.3.layers.3d) in the editor. You can also select a sticker and choose **Arrange → View in Layers**.

Layers opens in a sheet and lists your stickers from front to back. Drag the sheet up to see more layers. Use search to find a sticker by name, text or finish. Groups also appear when one of their stickers matches. Clear search to rearrange the list. Drag a row higher to bring it in front of other stickers, or lower to place it behind them.

![The Layers panel beside a burgundy photo collage, with individual photo and emoji rows and handles for changing their order.](asset:Explore/Layers/layersList)

Tap a row to return to the canvas with that sticker selected. This is especially useful when it is hidden behind another sticker. A group appears as one row, so you can rearrange all its stickers together. Tap **Done** to return to editing without choosing a row.

## Place stickers behind a photo subject

When photo **Depth** is enabled, the **Photo Subject** row separates stickers in front of the subject from those behind it. Move a sticker or group above that row to place it in front, or below to tuck it behind the subject.

## Make a quick adjustment

Select a sticker or group and open **Arrange**. **Move Forward** and **Move Backward** move it one step. **Bring to Front** and **Send to Back** move it all the way.

Tap ![Undo](symbol:arrow.uturn.backward) if you want to reverse a change.
