## Your sticker’s alive

Deco Live brings your stickers to macOS. The same sticker arrangement appears on your desktop and lock screen, adding a little of you to your Mac.

## A little movement, a little shimmer

Chrome, holographic, and glitter stickers react to your cursor, shifting their reflections as you move it.

On supported MacBook devices, they can react to the hinge too. Tilt the screen and watch the light change across your stickers.
