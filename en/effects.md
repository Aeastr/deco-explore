## A new look for a sticker

Give a sticker a different finish with **Filters**. Chrome makes it look reflective, Glitter adds a sparkle, and Holographic gives it shifting color. Try Sketch or Emboss for a different texture.

Choose a sticker, open **Filters**, and pick a look. Adjust **Intensity** to make it subtle or bold. Some effects also have a **Scale** control, so you can change the size of the pattern without resizing the sticker. Some filters require [Deco Plus](deco://plus/premium-finishes).
