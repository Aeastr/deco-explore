## Add a little motion

Chrome, holographic, and glitter stickers can add a gentle shimmer to a Live Photo wallpaper. In ![Export Wallpaper](symbol:square.and.arrow.down), choose a supported image size and turn on **Live Photo** to prepare an animated version. Live Photo export requires [Deco Plus](deco://plus/animated-wallpapers).

## Put it on your Lock Screen

Save the Live Photo to Photos, then choose it as your Lock Screen wallpaper. The motion you see depends on your artwork and your device's wallpaper settings.
